The evidence model

AI for reasoning. Code for correctness.

VERIDEX never asks you to trust a sentence. Every answer is broken into claims, and every claim is tied to evidence of a known kind.

How it works

Four steps, with a human making the final call.

01InvestigateThe AI plans the investigation and asks your data questions with safe read-only SQL.
02VerifyDeterministic code runs the queries, calculations, rankings and robustness checks.
03TraceEvery claim links to the evidence, SQL and rows behind it.
04ApproveYou review the evidence and robustness, then approve or reject.

Three kinds of evidence

Each piece of evidence says what it is and where it came from.

Fact

Observed directly

What it is

A value read straight from your data.

Produced by

A read-only SQL query.

Example

North generated $4.82M in revenue.
